\abstract{%
Imperial-era Chinese family genealogies (\emph{jiapu},
\begin{CJK*}{UTF8}{gbsn}家谱\end{CJK*}) preserve detailed records of
male descent yet systematically erase women: a wife's identity rarely
exceeds her surname, and a daughter's record terminates at her natal
lineage. The structural absence of marriage edges in jiapu obscures
the maternal kinship network --- the \emph{muxia}
(\begin{CJK*}{UTF8}{gbsn}母家\end{CJK*}, ``mother's house'') --- whose
role in late-imperial politics is well documented but quantitatively
under-studied. We introduce \system, a visual analytics workspace
that treats genealogical completion as an \emph{interpretable
link-prediction} task in which a heterogeneous graph transformer (HGT)
proposes candidate marriages and an LLM-driven multi-agent negotiation
re-litigates each proposal under historian-injectable constraints.
Because no jiapu corpus carries its own ground truth, we use the
CMGPD-LN demographic panel --- a separate Qing resource compiled from
triennial Banner population registers in Liaoning, which preserves
marriage and maternal edges that jiapu suppress --- as an evaluation
testbed: we ablate its $r_{hw}$ and $r_{md}/r_{ms}$ edges to simulate
the jiapu-style record gap, then grade reconstruction against the
held-out edges. On 131{,}140 held-out test pairs the HGT layer alone reaches
Hit@10 $= 0.624$ and MRR $= 0.428$ (ROC-AUC $= 0.99$),
yet unconstrained Hit@1 stays at $0.325$ --- well below a historian's
commit threshold even though per-cohort Hungarian assignment recovers
$62.6\%$ of marriages; \system\ closes the residual gap by routing
low-confidence cases through a six-round bilateral persona-grounded
negotiation, with the V4-batch operator-acceptance precision
calibrated to $\approx 0.87$ on the 1882 cohort (Hit@10 $= 0.841$,
MRR $= 0.633$ there), while preserving an audit trail that aligns
each commit with one or more SEAL motifs and (when available) sparse
archival hints. Three case studies demonstrate that \system\ converts
an opaque GNN ranking into a deliberative workflow whose every
accepted edge is justified by either a pattern in the graph, a story
in the agents' transcript, or both.
} % end of abstract

\keywords{Visual analytics, link prediction, heterogeneous graph
neural networks, large language models, multi-agent simulation,

\firstsection{Introduction}
\maketitle

Imperial-era Chinese family genealogies --- \emph{jiapu}
(\begin{CJK*}{UTF8}{gbsn}家谱\end{CJK*}) --- are the largest surviving
record of late-imperial kinship, but they are systematically lopsided:
patrilineal convention documents male descendants in detail while
reducing wives and daughters to a surname character or omitting them
outright. Edges between marriage partners ($r_{hw}$) and from a child
to their mother ($r_{md}, r_{ms}$) are therefore either absent,
surnames-only, or coarsely imputed across the jiapu corpus --- precisely
the substrate one would need to reconstruct the \emph{maternal kinship
network} that historians have long argued mediated political coalitions,
household labour allocation, and the flow of marriage-market resources
across banners~\cite{wang2007muxia, mann2002precious}.
The methodological gap this paper addresses is how to fill those
missing edges in a way a historian can defend, given that no jiapu
corpus carries its own ground truth.

Our solution is to use a \emph{separate} Qing-era resource --- the
China Multi-Generational Panel Dataset, Liaoning (CMGPD-LN,
1749--1909~\cite{cmgpd-ln-2010}), a demographic panel compiled from
triennial population registers maintained by the Banner administration
in Liaoning --- as an evaluation testbed. Unlike jiapu, CMGPD-LN
preserves the marriage and maternal edges. We \emph{simulate} the
jiapu completion task: ablate $r_{hw}$ and $r_{md}/r_{ms}$ from
CMGPD-LN to mimic the jiapu record gap, train on the ablated graph,
and grade reconstructions against the held-out edges that the panel
--- but no real jiapu --- preserves. The deployment target is real
jiapu; CMGPD-LN provides the methodology validation that jiapu
themselves cannot.

A heterogeneous graph transformer~\cite{hu2020hgt} together with a
SEAL-based subgraph-pattern matcher~\cite{zhang2018seal} provides a
strong distributional baseline. Two limitations motivate \system.
\emph{First}, even a well-tuned HGT exhibits a familiar
ranking-vs-decision gap: across 131k held-out pairs the true wife
appears within the top-10 in $62.4\%$ of cases
(Hit@10 $= 0.624$, MRR $= 0.428$) and the model is well-calibrated
(ROC-AUC $= 0.99$, PR-AUC $= 0.95$); per-cohort Hungarian assignment
recovers $62.6\%$ of marriages; yet \emph{unconstrained} Hit@1 stays
at $32.5\%$, well below a historian's commit threshold. An ablation
study reveals that maternal edges contribute $\approx\!0$ marginal
predictability above marriage edges
($\Delta$~Hungarian recall@1 $= -0.047$ vs.\ the unablated upper
bound), confirming the residual error is not absorbed by the graph
alone. \emph{Second}, the model is opaque; a probability
score offers no narrative purchase, and no surface for injecting the
\emph{partial} archival evidence (a surviving surname, a banner
constraint, an in-marriage event) that genealogists working with real
jiapu routinely have but cannot encode in a feature vector.

\system\ is our answer to both gaps. It is a six-panel visual
analytics workspace (\cref{fig:teaser}) built on top of the HGT+SEAL
backbone, designed around a single premise: \emph{a marriage edge
worth committing to a reconstructed jiapu must be defensible by either
a graph pattern, a deliberative narrative, or both, and never by a
black-box score alone}. \system\ couples the GNN ranking with an LLM
multi-agent negotiation in which each marriage-age individual on the
testbed is enrolled as an autonomous persona grounded in their CMGPD
life-event and income trajectory; a six-round one-to-many bilateral
protocol re-prices the HGT shortlist under round-by-round expert
hints; and an audit log preserves every commit with a multi-label
provenance tag (\textsf{HGT}, \textsf{MAS}, or both).

\noindent\textbf{Contributions.}
\textbf{(i)}~A visual analytics workspace pairing a per-cohort
honeycomb embedding view with an LLM negotiation arena, bridging GNN
link prediction on the CMGPD-LN testbed and historian-in-the-loop
reasoning, with the deployment target being real jiapu.
\textbf{(ii)}~An ablation-mirrored evaluation harness that simulates
the jiapu record gap directly on the demographic panel by removing
every $r_{hw}$ and $r_{md}/r_{ms}$ edge prior to training --- a
benchmark no jiapu corpus can supply on its own.
\textbf{(iii)}~A six-round bilateral negotiation protocol whose final
ranking obeys $\mathrm{score}_i = \tfrac12 (s_i + t_i)
- \lambda\,|s_i - t_i|$, with hint injection drained between rounds.
\textbf{(iv)}~A multi-label provenance scheme on every committed edge
that distinguishes HGT-batch acceptances from MAS-overrides and flags
the convergent case, supporting cumulative-error diagnosis through
one-click restore.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{VA for relational and historical archives.}
Network-completion visualisations for biographical data have long
combined edge-uncertainty cues with mixed-initiative
exploration~\cite{vis-prosopography};
recent work treats link prediction as the analytic primitive and
examines analyst response to model
confidence~\cite{vis-link-prediction-confidence}. We extend this
tradition by committing explicitly to GNN-based ranking as the surface
from which exploration begins, and by treating an LLM agent transcript
as a first-class evidence layer rather than as an external explanation.
\textbf{Heterogeneous graph learning.} HGT~\cite{hu2020hgt} extends
attention across typed relations; SEAL~\cite{zhang2018seal} exposes
the local structural pattern motivating a prediction. We use both as
backbone and treat their outputs as priors for an LLM agent population.
\textbf{LLM agents and negotiation.}
Generative agents have framed LLMs as deliberative
units~\cite{park2023generative}, and negotiation protocols expose
persona-grounded reasoning~\cite{abdelnabi2023negotiation}; we
specialise this line by seeding agents with DS0003 life-history
evidence and SEAL motif tags. \textbf{Mixed-initiative
systems}~\cite{endert2014mixed} ground our hint console: the
\texttt{@everyone}, \texttt{@target}, and \texttt{@c-XX} addresses are
drained between rounds so historians can inject brittle archival
evidence without breaking the agent loop.

\section{Domain Background and Task Abstraction}
\label{sec:domain}

\textbf{The target gap (jiapu).} In jiapu the patriline is the unit of
record: each male descendant is a generation entry in the lineage
book, while a female descendant typically appears at most as a
surname on her father's entry, and is often omitted entirely upon
marriage. The \emph{muxia} ---
\begin{CJK*}{UTF8}{gbsn}母家\end{CJK*}, the maternal household to which
a wife traces her natal lineage --- is therefore structurally invisible
in the source. Reconstructing it from jiapu alone is impossible
because the very edges we wish to recover are the ones the source
omits.

\textbf{The empirical handle (CMGPD-LN).} We instead use CMGPD-LN
(ICPSR 27063) as a testbed. CMGPD-LN is a Qing demographic and
administrative panel compiled from the triennial population registers
maintained by the Banner administration in Liaoning between 1749 and
1909~\cite{cmgpd-ln-2010}; \emph{unlike} jiapu it preserves the
$r_{hw}$ and $r_{md}/r_{ms}$ edges along with individual life-history
records (DS0003 events + estimated income). We exploit this asymmetry
methodologically: ablate $r_{hw}, r_{md}, r_{ms}$ from CMGPD-LN,
train the reconstruction system on the ablated graph, and grade
against the panel's held-out ground truth.

\textbf{Tasks.} Formative interviews with three domain experts (E1,
demographic historian; E2, computational genealogist; E3, digital-
humanities methodologist) yielded:
\textbf{T1} identify high-confidence candidates within a cohort year,
with awareness of the kinship motifs underpinning each;
\textbf{T2} adjudicate uncertain candidates by injecting partial
archival evidence and inspecting the agents' reasoning; and
\textbf{T3} audit the cumulative reconstruction with a per-edge
provenance trail and reversal mechanism.
These distil into six design goals --- \textbf{DG1 cohort-as-canvas,
DG2 provenance-by-construction, DG3 narrative grounding, DG4
steerable deliberation, DG5 reversibility, DG6 tight coupling} ---
which govern every view in \system.

\section{Computational Backbone}
\label{sec:backbone}

\textbf{Graph schema and ablation.} Let
$G = (V, E, \mathcal{T}, \mathcal{R})$ denote the heterogeneous
graph induced by CMGPD-LN with node types
$\mathcal{T} = \{\textsf{person}, \textsf{household},
\textsf{community}, \textsf{banner}\}$ and nine edge types
$\mathcal{R}$ including patrilineal kinship ($r_{fs}, r_{fd}$),
matrilineal kinship ($r_{ms}, r_{md}$), co-residence
($r_{hh}, r_{hc}, r_{cb}$), and timed marriage edges $r_{hw}$
carrying $\textsf{edge\_time} = y$. The \emph{jiapu-mirroring
ablation} replaces, for $r \in \{r_{hw}, r_{ms}, r_{md}\}$, the edge
index tensor with an empty $2 \times 0$ tensor while preserving the
relation type in $G.\mathrm{metadata}()$ --- an ``ablate, don't delete''
invariant required because per-relation projections are registered on
the metadata snapshot.

\textbf{HGT encoder.} Each layer
updates $h_v^{(\ell+1)}$ by attention over typed neighbours
$(u, r) \in \mathcal{N}(v)$:
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{u,v}^{r} = \mathrm{softmax}_u\!\left(
  \tfrac{(W^{q}_{\tau(v)} h_v^{(\ell)})^{\!\top}
         W^{r}_{\mathrm{att}}
         (W^{k}_{\tau(u)} h_u^{(\ell)})}{\sqrt{d}}
  \cdot \mu_{\tau(u),r,\tau(v)}\right)
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
h_v^{(\ell+1)} = \mathrm{LN}\!\Bigl(
  h_v^{(\ell)} + \!\sum_{r,u} \alpha_{u,v}^{r}\,
    W^{r}_{\mathrm{msg}}\,W^{v}_{\tau(u)} h_u^{(\ell)}\Bigr)
\end{equation}
where $\tau(\cdot)$ returns node type and $\mu$ is a learned
relation-prior tensor. After two layers a marriage scorer
$\psi(h_m, h_w) = \mathrm{MLP}([h_m, h_w, |h_m - h_w|, h_m \odot h_w])$
emits a logit for every candidate pair; negatives are sampled
within-cohort to prevent cross-year shortcuts.

\textbf{SEAL motifs.} For each candidate $(m, w)$ we extract the
$k$-hop enclosing subgraph $\mathcal{N}_k(m, w)$ and apply
Double-Radius Node Labelling (DRNL) to produce a structural
fingerprint~\cite{zhang2018seal}. We pre-define four canonical
motifs of historical interest: $\mathsf{m}_1$ \emph{father-brother},
$\mathsf{m}_2$ \emph{uncle-in-law}, $\mathsf{m}_3$
\emph{same-household}, $\mathsf{m}_4$ \emph{banner-endogamy}; a
candidate matches $\mathsf{m}_k$ when its DRNL signature is consistent
with the exemplar's labelled subgraph. The matched motif set is
surfaced verbatim in the agent's persona prompt (\cref{sec:mas}).

\textbf{Bilateral final score.} Given a husband-side score
$t_i \in [0, 10]$ and a wife-side counter-score $s_i \in [0, 10]$
at the close of round 5, the round-6 ranking obeys
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{score}_i = \tfrac{1}{2} (s_i + t_i)
  - \lambda\,|s_i - t_i|, \qquad \lambda = 0.3.
\label{eq:final}
\end{equation}
This rewards mutual-high configurations while penalising asymmetric
agreements; $\lambda$ is exposed in V6.

\section{\system: Visual Analytics Design}
\label{sec:design}

\system\ is a 3-column $\times$ 2-row grid of six linked views, backed
by a FastAPI server with WebSocket fan-out for the agent stream
(\cref{fig:teaser}). The frontend (Vue 3 + Vite) holds an in-memory
cohort context and routes selection events through a shared bus
(\texttt{hex-select}, \texttt{person-selected},
\texttt{match-accepted}, \texttt{match-restored}). The backend caches
the cleaned CMGPD-LN parquet, the HGT model checkpoint, and DS0003
per-person life histories.

\textbf{V1 --- Acceptance curve.} The $x$-axis advances in cumulative
\emph{accepted-edge} ticks; the $y$-axis plots running recall@1 of
operator commits against the cohort's known ground truth. A
horizontal reference line marks the static HGT-only baseline,
computed via per-cohort Hungarian assignment. The step function
makes each commit's contribution visible (DG2).

\textbf{V2 --- Processed pairs with multi-label provenance.} Every
accepted match becomes a row carrying husband and wife IDs, HGT
score, score gap, the Hungarian verdict, and \system's core artefact:
a \emph{source} chip set. \emph{Rules:} a V4-batch commit receives
\textsf{HGT} (sage); a V5-MAS commit receives \textsf{MAS} (amber);
when the MAS-chosen wife coincides with the husband's HGT
$\arg\max$, the row carries \emph{both} chips, signalling convergent
agreement. A trailing \emph{restore} button issues
\texttt{POST /negotiate/\{id\}/restore}, removes the entry from the
accept log, and broadcasts \texttt{match-restored}, causing V1's
curve to recompute, V3's masked dot to reappear, and V4's bipartite
edge to re-enter selection (DG5).

\textbf{V3 --- Honeycomb embedding canvas.} V3 carries DG1. Pairs are
projected into 2-D via per-cohort metric MDS on the HGT joint
embedding and binned into a flat-top hex grid via an iterative
inward-attraction packer (capacity 12 pairs per cell). Three modes:
\texttt{honeycomb} (cell colour encodes mean signed score gap on a
divergent ramp anchored at $\pm 2$ logits, with cluster borders
between hexes of differing cluster IDs); \texttt{scatter} (one dot
per pair, percentile-clipped 2nd--98th MDS coordinates); and
\texttt{mixed} (hexes dimmed to 0.55 with dots overlaid). V3
\emph{masks} dots whose $(h, w)$ is in the running accept set; the
canvas thus reflects ``what is left to commit'' at any point.

\textbf{V4 --- Bipartite detail and batch.} V4 unfolds V3's
selection into a husband-on-left, candidate-on-right bipartite, with
B\'ezier edges weighted by HGT score. A \emph{profile popup}
(banner labelled \emph{Solid White}, region labelled
\emph{South Liaoning}) opens on any node click. A \emph{batch}
commit accepts every husband's HGT $\arg\max$ above a configurable
score-gap threshold (default 1.0, $\approx$\,87\% precision on the
1882 cohort), all surfacing in V2 with the \textsf{HGT}-only chip.
A husband-node click also emits \texttt{person-selected}, which V5
picks up to load that husband as the negotiation target.

\textbf{V5 --- The agent arena.} V5 is the deliberative core (DG3,
DG4). After a husband loads, V5 displays a collapsible
\emph{life-history} panel with the LLM-produced persona headline,
matched SEAL motif chips, an event pill strip (e.g., 1864
\emph{Birth}, 1880 \emph{In-Marriage}) and a terraced income
trajectory. The candidate grid renders one card per top-$K$ wife,
each with persona summary, round-by-round score pips
(\textsf{R$N$:t$X$/c$Y$}) for $t_i$ and $s_i$, and a chevron that
expands the full per-round transcript. The \emph{hint console}
above the grid accepts free-form messages addressed to
\texttt{@everyone}, \texttt{@target}, or \texttt{@c-XX}; submitted
hints land in a per-husband \texttt{asyncio.Queue} on the server,
are drained between rounds, and are interpolated into the next
round's prompt as system context.

\textbf{V6 --- Rule injector.} A small panel of macro sliders
(paternal-lineage importance, sibling overlap, household share,
banner match, macro era) re-weights the SEAL motif pre-prior; four
boolean toggles let the analyst suppress
$\mathsf{m}_1$--$\mathsf{m}_4$; a $\lambda$ input exposes
\cref{eq:final} sensitivity.

\textbf{Linked interaction.} The four bus events form the backbone of
inter-view coordination (DG6). On \texttt{match-accepted}: V2
prepends, V1 reloads, V3 masks, V4 drops the edge. On
\texttt{match-restored}: the symmetric reverse. This single source of
truth is what allows \system\ to behave as a single instrument
rather than as a collection of widgets.

\section{Multi-Agent Negotiation Protocol}
\label{sec:mas}

\textbf{Persona instantiation.} Each persona prompt is rendered with:
profile (sex, birth year, banner-affiliation \emph{label}, geographic
region \emph{label}, community ID, household ID); the HGT pre-score
against the husband; an \emph{event block} formatted as
\texttt{\{year\}: \{event\_1\_label\} / \{event\_2\_label\}}
constructed from DS0003 EVENT\_1/2 codes substituted via
\texttt{event\_value\_labels.json} (e.g., 1=Death, 7=In-Marriage,
13=Returned-after-absconding); an \emph{income block} formatted as
\texttt{\{year\}: \{income\} (\{level\})} bucketed at the cohort's
33rd/66th percentiles; the SEAL motif tag set; and any hints
addressed to this role. The LLM returns a JSON object with four
fields --- \texttt{headline}, \texttt{traits}, \texttt{values},
\texttt{red\_flags} --- which the client renders verbatim as chips.

\textbf{Round structure (\cref{alg:rounds}).}
Round 1 generates personas for the husband and all $K$ candidates in
parallel. Rounds 2--5 walk through canonical foci
(\emph{impressions}, \emph{deep-dive}, \emph{rebuttals},
\emph{alignment}); each round is a single batched query call from
the husband (returning per-candidate scores $t_i$ and one pointed
question per candidate) followed by $K$ parallel candidate answer
calls (each returning $a_{c,r}$ and counter-score $s_{c,r}$).
Round 6 computes \cref{eq:final} from the round-5 scores and
publishes the \emph{final\_ranking}. Critically, every round
terminates at a \texttt{round\_paused} frame and the orchestrator
suspends on an \texttt{asyncio.Event} that is set only when the
operator clicks \emph{Approve \& Advance}; this gating realises DG4.
Per-call \texttt{try}/\texttt{except} falls back to a deterministic
heuristic so the protocol remains complete even when the LLM
endpoint errors.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Six-round bilateral negotiation.}
\label{alg:rounds}
\small
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require husband $h$, candidate set $C$, cohort year $y$, ablation $a$
\State $\pi_h \gets \texttt{chat\_json(persona|}h\texttt{)}$;
       publish \texttt{persona}, motifs $\emptyset$
\ForAll{$c \in C$ in parallel}
  \State $\pi_c \gets \texttt{chat\_json(persona|}c\texttt{)}$;
         publish \texttt{persona}, motifs $\mathcal{M}(h, c)$
\EndFor
\State \textbf{wait} \texttt{advance\_event}; drain hint queue
\For{round $r = 2, 3, 4, 5$}
  \State $(S_r, Q_r) \gets \texttt{chat\_json(query|}h, \pi_h,
     \{\pi_c\}, r\texttt{)}$
  \ForAll{$c \in C$ in parallel}
    \State $(a_{c,r}, s_{c,r}) \gets
       \texttt{chat\_json(answer|}c, \pi_c, \pi_h, q_{c,r}, r\texttt{)}$
    \State publish \texttt{query}, \texttt{answer}
  \EndFor
  \State publish \texttt{round\_scores}; \texttt{round\_paused};
         wait; drain hints
\EndFor
\State Compute $\mathrm{score}_i$ per \cref{eq:final}; rank
\State publish \texttt{final\_ranking}
\State \textbf{on user accept}: \texttt{commit\_match}; broadcast
       \texttt{match-accepted}
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\section{Use Cases}
\label{sec:cases}

We illustrate three scenarios drawn from formative sessions with
E1--E3.

\textbf{UC1: cohort triage of the 1882 marriages (E1).}
E1 opens the 1882 ablated cohort from the CMGPD-LN testbed. V3 shows
a tight cluster of green hexes; she clicks one, V4 unfolds the
8-husband bipartite (all score gaps above 2.0), and a batch commit
at threshold 1.0 commits 7 of 8 in one stroke (\textsf{HGT} chip).
The remaining husband routes to V5; the persona reveals an income
trajectory that flattened in 1879 and a single \emph{Lost} event in
1881. E1, recognising this as a known absconding pattern, types
\texttt{@everyone be skeptical of stability claims} into the hint
console; rounds 3--5 reflect the directive in their queries and
final scores. The negotiation converges on a different wife than
HGT's $\arg\max$, and the row in V2 acquires \emph{both}
\textsf{HGT} and \textsf{MAS} chips on acceptance, recording the
divergence between the two pipelines.

\textbf{UC2: probing the maternal-edge ablation (E3).}
E3 toggles between an \texttt{ablated} cohort (only $r_{hw}$
suppressed) and the \texttt{unablated} graph; the honeycomb geometry
visibly shifts and clusters reorganise around banner co-membership.
Suppressing $\mathsf{m}_4$ in V6 and re-running a single husband's
negotiation in V5 changes the match --- the system surfaces the
motif-level dependency rather than absorbing it silently.

\textbf{UC3: real-jiapu evidence injection (E2).}
E2 simulates the deployment scenario: she uses \system\ \emph{as if}
the cohort were a real jiapu with only the wife's surname
(\emph{Wang}) recorded, while the underlying data still has hidden
ground truth available for grading. After round 1 she types
\texttt{@everyone wife's surname is Wang} into the hint console;
candidates whose surname does not match are surfaced with reduced
$t_i$ scores in their explanations, and the surname-Wang candidate's
persona is reasoned about explicitly in the next round's transcript.
The final commit's chip is \textsf{MAS}-only --- HGT's $\arg\max$
was a different wife --- and V2's audit trail records the divergence.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{Generalisation beyond the testbed.}
\system\ is validated on CMGPD-LN's ablation harness, but the
deployment target is real jiapu, where ground truth is unavailable
and per-person evidence is sparser. Our event- and income-block
formatters degrade gracefully (rendering \texttt{(no events on
register)} when DS0003 is empty), but field-level ablation studies
on synthetic-sparsity panels are an immediate next step.
\textbf{LLM provenance auditability.} The per-round transcript is
preserved verbatim; per-call \texttt{try}/\texttt{except} +
heuristic fallback isolates the orchestrator from any single bad
call, but a fully audited corpus would require attaching cryptographic
attestations to each prompt+response pair, which we do not do.
\textbf{Monocultural priors.} The Qing-era persona prompt embeds
banner endogamy and lineage continuity explicitly; reuse for a
Korean, Vietnamese, or Han-elsewhere lineage tradition would require
redesigning these defaults --- DG3 was deliberately written to surface
this so the prompt templates remain a first-class artefact of the
design.
\textbf{Compute envelope.} A six-round real-Qwen negotiation for six
candidates costs $\approx$\,30--45\,s on the DashScope
\texttt{qwen-plus-2025-04-28} endpoint; round-1 personas and
round-2--5 answers dispatch in parallel via
\texttt{asyncio.gather}, so the interface remains responsive.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\system\ reframes genealogical marriage-edge reconstruction as a
collaborative analytic process between an HGT link-prediction
backbone, a SEAL motif-grounded LLM agent population, and a historian
whose archival hints are first-class inputs to the loop. By making
provenance, narrative, and reversibility constitutive of every commit
--- not after-the-fact explanations --- \system\ provides a workspace
in which low-confidence GNN rankings become defensible reconstructions.
We validate on CMGPD-LN, whose ablation harness lets each design
decision be graded against ground truth; the deployment target is
real jiapu, where ground truth is unavailable but the female-record
gap is the actual problem \system\ is built around. The system
generalises in the direction that matters: every artefact remains
interpretable to the historian who must vouch for the finished tree.
